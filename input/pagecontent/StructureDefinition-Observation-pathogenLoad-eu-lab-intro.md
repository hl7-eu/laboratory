The load of a pathogen in a specimen, measured by quantitative nucleic acid
amplification: a concentration in IU/mL or copies/mL, a value outside the
measuring range with its comparator (e.g. `< 35` IU/mL when detected below the
limit of quantification), or *Not detected*. The technique – real-time PCR,
digital PCR or transcription-mediated amplification – is given in `method`.

The conventions and the examples are described in the
[Quantification](microbiology.html#quantification) section of the [Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
