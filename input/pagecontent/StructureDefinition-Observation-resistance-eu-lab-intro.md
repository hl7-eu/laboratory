The detection of a resistance mechanism or gene: an enzyme such as a
carbapenemase, a mechanism such as an extended-spectrum beta-lactamase, or a
gene such as *mecA*. The result is `Detected` or `Not detected`, one
observation per target. The technique – PCR, rapid immunoassay, mCIM or a
phenotypic confirmation test – is given in `method`; a mechanism read from a
phenotypic test is reported with the technique of that test. The observation
points back at what caused the test through `triggeredBy`.

The conventions and the examples are described in the
[Resistance](microbiology.html#resistance) section of the [Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
