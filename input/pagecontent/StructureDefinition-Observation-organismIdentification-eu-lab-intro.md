The identification of a microorganism without a predefined target: the code
reflects the open question, the value is the organism, the method names the
technique — MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry, microscopy, sequencing. The
identification is a step of its own, separate from the culture whose growth it
names, and points back at that culture through `triggeredBy`.

Where a predefined target is sought and the answer is whether it is present, the
examination is a
[targeted detection](StructureDefinition-Observation-targetedDetection-eu-lab.html).

The conventions and the examples are described on the
[Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
