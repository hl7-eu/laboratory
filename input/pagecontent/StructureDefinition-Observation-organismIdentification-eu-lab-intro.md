The identification of a microorganism without a predefined target: the code
reflects the open question, the value is the organism, the method names the
technique — MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry, microscopy, sequencing. The
identification is a step of its own, separate from the
[general culture](StructureDefinition-Observation-cultureGeneral-eu-lab.html)
whose growth it names, and points back at that culture through `triggeredBy`.

Where a predefined target is sought and the answer is whether it is present,
the examination is a
[targeted detection](StructureDefinition-Observation-targetedDetection-eu-lab.html)
or, by microscopy, a
[specific microscopy](StructureDefinition-Observation-microscopySpecific-eu-lab.html).

The conventions and the examples are described in the
[Identification](microbiology.html#identification) section of the [Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
