The specimen examined in microbiology: the primary clinical specimen, the
material taken from a positive culture, or an isolate. Two things the
microbiology conventions place on the specimen rather than on the observation:

* **the staining**, as a processing step with a procedure from
  `37926009 |Microbial stain method|` — the preparation of the material, kept
  apart from the microscopy technique in `Observation.method`;
* **the derivation**, through `parent` — the Gram stain of a positive blood
  culture or of an isolate references the derived specimen, which points at the
  primary specimen.

The conventions and the examples are described on the
[Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
