The general culture: does anything grow? The code is `11475-1`, the value
*No growth* or *Organism growth*, and the method gives the culture condition,
aerobic or anaerobic. The organism that grew is named by the
[organism identification](StructureDefinition-Observation-organismIdentification-eu-lab.html)
that follows, which points back at the culture through `triggeredBy`.

The conventions and the examples are described in the
[Culture](microbiology.html#culture) section of the [Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
