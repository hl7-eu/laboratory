The antibiogram of an isolate. It groups one susceptibility result per
antimicrobial through `hasMember` and points back at the identification of the
organism tested through `triggeredBy`. It has no value and no interpretation of
its own; each member carries its result, its interpretation with the standard,
and its method.

The conventions and the examples are described in the
[Susceptibility](microbiology.html#susceptibility) section of the [Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
