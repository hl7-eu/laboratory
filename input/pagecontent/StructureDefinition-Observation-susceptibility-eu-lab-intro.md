The susceptibility of an isolate to one antimicrobial. The result is
preferably the minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) or the zone diameter; a
value outside the tested range carries the comparator, e.g. `> 8` mg/L. The
category S, I, R is the interpretation, with the standard and the version of
its breakpoint table; where only the category is available, it is also the
value, with the same code. The method is the technique, e.g. MIC or disk
diffusion.

The conventions and the examples are described in the
[Susceptibility](microbiology.html#susceptibility) section of the [Microbiology](microbiology.html) page.
